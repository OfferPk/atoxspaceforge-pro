\#\# 2\. 10-Point Normative Compliance Checklist  
1\. ATOX-01: Minimum Residual Thickness Safety Margin (M \>= \+33.33%, SF \>= 1.50) per ECSS-E-ST-10-04C Cl. 9.3.  
2\. ATOX-02: Flight-Screened Erosion Yield (E\_y \<= 3.00e-24 cm3/atom) per ASTM E2089-15.  
3\. ATOX-03: Ram Atomic Oxygen Cumulative Fluence Envelope (F\_AO \<= 1.00e23 atoms/cm2).  
4\. ATOX-04: Protective Coating Pinhole Defect Fraction (chi\_defect \<= 0.050, \<= 5.0% defect area) per ISO 15856:2014.  
5\. ATOX-05: Solar Cell Interconnect Silver Oxidation Protection (Encapsulated or Non-Reactive) per NASA TM-106427.  
6\. ATOX-06: Polymeric Thermal Radiator Solar Absorptance Shift (Delta alpha\_s \<= 0.0500) per ECSS-E-ST-31C.  
7\. ATOX-07: Structural Composite Load Path Carbon-Erosion Depth (dx\_CFRP \<= 2.00 microns) per NASA-STD-6016.  
8\. ATOX-08: Mass Loss Per Unit Surface Area (dm/A \<= 5.00 mg/cm2).  
9\. ATOX-09: Spacecraft LEO Orbital Altitude Operating Window (h \>= 180.0 km).  
10\. ATOX-10: Thermospheric Solar Activity Range (F\_10.7 \<= 250.0 sfu).
